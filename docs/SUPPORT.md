# The Free I Ching support

Email [morphiclabsdata@gmail.com](mailto:morphiclabsdata@gmail.com) for help,
feedback, purchase questions, or privacy requests about The Free I Ching.

Include your app version, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.
You do not need to send private journal entries or reflections to get help.

For a purchase issue, describe what happened during purchase or restoration.
Do not email payment card details or account passwords.
