# Third-party text

The changing-line text in `src/data/classicalLines.json` is derived from
[`jesshewitt/i-ching`](https://github.com/jesshewitt/i-ching), where the
English translation is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
Universal.

That project describes the text as a 2026 English translation from Richard
Wilhelm's public-domain 1924 German edition, translated by Claude (Anthropic)
and edited by Jess Hewitt. Only the six standard line texts for each hexagram
are included here.

The contemporary judgment, image, and counsel in
`src/lib/hexagramInterpretations.js` are original paraphrases written for this
application. They are interpretation, not quotations from a canonical
translation.
