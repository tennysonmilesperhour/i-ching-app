# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People seeking a calm, private way to consult the I Ching, understand a reading at their preferred depth, and return to reflections over time. The primary task is to pose a question, cast a hexagram, and sit with useful guidance without commercial pressure or unnecessary ceremony.

## Product Purpose

The Free I Ching provides a transparent, accessible oracle with Inquirer and Adept reading roles, a searchable hexagram library, and an optional private journal. Success means the service remains free to use, the casting method and source material are clear, and people can move from a reading into thoughtful reflection.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, contemplative, and trustworthy. The voice should be warm without sentimentality, direct without feeling clinical, and respectful of both the source tradition and the user's own judgment.

## Anti-references

Avoid fortune-telling theatrics, mystical visual clichés, manipulative engagement patterns, loud monetization, interruptive advertising, and any support request that implies payment affects the quality or availability of a reading.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the reading at the center of every screen.
2. Make depth optional: offer guidance first and detail on demand.
3. Explain methods and sources plainly so the experience earns trust.
4. Treat support as a quiet invitation after value has been received.
5. Preserve continuity across web and native experiences without disguising platform conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, comfortable touch targets, responsive text sizing, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone, and keep the core reading flow usable without animation or payment.
